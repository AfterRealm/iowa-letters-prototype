What the map shows

The six letters in the prototype span the western half of the Civil War theater. The 16th Iowa Volunteer Infantry was raised in early 1862 and trained at Camp McClellan in Davenport. The regiment then moved south through Memphis to the Vicksburg campaign, on through the Atlanta campaign in 1864, the March to the Sea, and finally the Carolinas Campaign in early 1865. Every place plotted on this map corresponds to a real movement of an Iowa regiment in the war.

The descriptive metadata in Omeka stores the place name (under dcterms:spatial) as a literal string. The mapping from that string to a coordinate happens in the presentation layer, against a curated gazetteer. That separation is intentional: it keeps the descriptive metadata clean (descriptive metadata should describe, not encode geometry), and it lets the same gazetteer feed both the map view here and the autocomplete in the authoring UI.

Full details of the data pipeline, the gazetteer authority pattern, and the accessibility approach for an interactive map are on the Map Methodology page.
